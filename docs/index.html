EFO-Plutchik 0.5.0

An EFO (Emotion Frames Ontology) compatible module formalizing Plutchik's wheel of emotions: 8 basic emotions with their wheel positions, adjacencies and 4 opposition pairs, 16 intensity variants (low / high), 24 primary, secondary and tertiary dyads, a vocabulary for valence-arousal-dominance readings, and derivation rules for inferring compound emotions from intensity readings.

Classes

Basic Emotion 基本感情

plu:BasicEmotion · owl:Class

One of the eight primary emotions of Plutchik's wheel (Joy, Trust, Fear, Surprise, Sadness, Disgust, Anger, Anticipation), arranged in four bipolar opposition pairs; each is the medium-intensity form of its petal.

Plutchik の wheel の 8 つの基本感情（喜び・信頼・恐れ・驚き・悲しみ・嫌悪・怒り・期待）。4 組の対極をなし、それぞれが花弁の中程度の強度にあたる。

owl:disjointWith
plu:CompoundEmotion
rdfs:subClassOf
plu:PlutchikEmotion

Compound Emotion 複合感情

plu:CompoundEmotion · owl:Class

An emotion that arises from the combination of two or more basic emotions.

2 つ以上の基本感情の組み合わせから生じる感情。

rdfs:subClassOf
plu:PlutchikEmotion

Derivation Rule 導出規則

plu:DerivationRule · owl:Class

A rule that derives compound-emotion occurrences from basic-emotion intensity readings, parameterised by an activation threshold.

基本感情の強度の読み取り値から複合感情の事例を導く規則。活性化の閾値をパラメータに持つ。

Dyad dyad（二項複合感情）

plu:Dyad · owl:Class

A compound emotion formed by combining exactly two basic emotions that are not opposites.

対極でない 2 つの基本感情をちょうど組み合わせてできる複合感情。

rdfs:subClassOf
plu:CompoundEmotion

Intensity Level 強度段階

plu:IntensityLevel · owl:Class

One of the three ordered intensity levels of Plutchik's wheel (low, medium, high), corresponding to its outer, middle and inner rings.

Plutchik の wheel の順序づいた 3 つの強度段階（弱・中・強）。外側・中間・内側の環に対応する。

Intensity Reading 強度の読み取り値

plu:IntensityReading · owl:Class

A quantified measurement of the intensity of a Plutchik emotion within an emotion situation.

感情の状況における Plutchik の感情の強度を数値で測ったもの。

Intensity Variant 強度の変種

plu:IntensityVariant · owl:Class

A low- or high-intensity form of a basic emotion on Plutchik's wheel (e.g. serenity and ecstasy for joy).

Plutchik の wheel における基本感情の弱い形または強い形（例: 喜びに対する平穏と恍惚）。

plu:PlutchikEmotion

Plutchik Emotion Plutchik の感情

plu:PlutchikEmotion · owl:Class

Any emotion in Plutchik's model of affect; the common superclass of basic emotions, their intensity variants and compound emotions.

Plutchik の感情モデルに含まれる感情。基本感情・その強度の変種・複合感情の共通の上位クラス。

wheel position wheel 上の位置

plu:wheelPosition · owl:DatatypeProperty, owl:FunctionalProperty

The clockwise position (0-7) of a basic emotion on Plutchik's wheel, starting from joy; the circular distance between two positions (1, 2, 3 or 4) determines whether they form a primary, secondary or tertiary dyad or are opposites.

Plutchik の wheel 上の基本感情の時計回りの位置（喜びを 0 とする 0〜7）。2 つの位置の円周上の距離（1・2・3・4）で、一次・二次・三次 dyad か対極かが決まる。

rdfs:range
